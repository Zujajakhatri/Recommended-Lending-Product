Prices roughly doubled between 2020 and 2025. In nominal terms personal loans look almost flat, but in 2020 prices they fell from PKR 282.9 billion to PKR 132.2 billion, more than halving. Vehicle finance fell from PKR 256.5 billion to PKR 159.4 billion in real terms.

  • 2022–2023 was the turning point. Inflation reached 19.9% and then 30.8%, interest rates rose, and vehicle finance fell 24.7% in 2023.
  • Only home finance and credit cards beat inflation. Their real growth was 3.3% and 8.8% a year.
  • 2025 shows a recovery. With inflation at 3.6% in 2025, vehicle finance grew 35.5% and personal loans 10.7% in nominal terms.

The fall in personal loans reflects lower supply, not lower demand. Banks cut unsecured lending when inflation and rates were high. As conditions ease, this leaves a large under-served gap.

  • Large banks do not prioritise consumer lending. Conventional banks put only 4.4% of their advances into it.
  • The digital model works. Easypaisa (the only digital bank) puts 68.9% of its advances into consumer finance and grew its book 36.2% a year, the fastest of any bank type.
  • Microfinance banks are gaining share (6.9% to 10.1%), showing strong demand for small-ticket loans.

5 Recommendation

The DRB should launch with digital personal loans, then add vehicle finance and home finance as its deposit base and partnerships grow.

1

Digital Personal Loans · Year 1

Salary advance and small-ticket loans, applied for, approved and repaid in the app, with a Shariah-compliant option.

2

Vehicle Finance · Year 2

Car and motorbike finance through dealer partnerships, launched once interest rates and the deposit base are stable.

3

Home Finance · Year 3+

Islamic home finance through partners or government schemes, once long-term funding is in place.

+

Digital Card / Buy Now, Pay Later

Credit card financing grew 25% a year. A digital card fits naturally next to personal loans.

Why now: inflation fell to 3.6% in 2025, personal loans returned to growth, and banks are still lending less than half of their deposits. A digital bank with low servicing costs can win this under-served segment.
